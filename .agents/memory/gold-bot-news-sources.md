---
name: Gold Bot News Sources
description: Reliability rules for sourcing and labeling gold-market headlines.
---

Use gold-specific RSS queries and reject headlines that are not clearly about gold, XAU/USD, bullion, or precious metals. Do not label broad finance search results as gold news.

**Why:** Yahoo symbol RSS/search responses were observed returning unrelated equities, while aggregator results can also include chart pages and user commentary.

**How to apply:** Keep freshness limits, publisher filtering, duplicate removal, and article links. If no verified recent stories remain, show no headlines rather than filling the card with generic finance news.
