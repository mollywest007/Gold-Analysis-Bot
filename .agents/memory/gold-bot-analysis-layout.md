---
name: Gold Bot Analysis Layout
description: Durable presentation rules for Telegram analysis and entry decision panels.
---

The Telegram analysis experience uses a fixed reading order in one analysis message: market snapshot and decision summary first, strict confirmed-entry gate and plan second, early-watch/provisional plan third, then institutional evidence, market context, blockers, and invalidation.

**Why:** Mixing the early-watch checklist into the institutional score made it difficult to distinguish a manual provisional setup from a confirmed active-trade plan, while sending a second board made the analysis feel fragmented.

**How to apply:** Keep strict confirmation labeled as confirmed or waiting, keep provisional levels labeled manual-only and non-active, and preserve the single combined card below Telegram's message limit.
